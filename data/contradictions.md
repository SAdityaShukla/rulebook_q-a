# Planted Contradictions

These three contradictions are intentional benchmark fixtures. Each pair makes incompatible claims about the same practical situation and has stable citation anchors.

## C1 — Laboratory attendance waiver
- `academic_regulations.md#1.4`: laboratory attendance is mandatory and says no exception or committee review is available below 80%.
- `academic_regulations.md#3.2`: says the Academic Review Committee can waive the 80% laboratory attendance requirement in exceptional circumstances.
- Conflict: one clause denies committee review while the other explicitly grants committee waiver authority.

## C2 — Approved fee extension
- `academic_regulations.md#17.1`: says an approved fee extension suspends late-fee calculation for the approved period.
- `academic_regulations.md#17.2`: says late fees continue throughout the extension period, including after approval, until semester end.
- Conflict: the same approved extension period is simultaneously described as fee-free and as continuing to accrue late fees.

## C3 — Merit scholarship and attendance waiver
- `academic_regulations.md#18.1`: requires at least 75% attendance in every enrolled course and says an attendance waiver does not alter scholarship eligibility.
- `academic_regulations.md#18.2`: says an approved attendance waiver may satisfy the attendance condition for Merit Scholarship purposes.
- Conflict: one provision preserves the 75% condition despite a waiver, while the other treats the waiver as satisfying that condition.
