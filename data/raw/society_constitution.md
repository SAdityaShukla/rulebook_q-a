# Student Society Constitution

## 1. Name and Purpose
A student society operates for an educational, technical, cultural, professional, or community purpose approved by the institution. The society must maintain a constitution, identify its faculty coordinator, and conduct activities consistently with institutional rules.

## 2. Membership
Membership is open according to the eligibility conditions stated in the society's approved constitution. Membership records should be maintained and material eligibility conditions should be communicated clearly to members.

## 3. Office Bearers
The society shall have the office-bearers specified in its approved constitution. Office bearers are responsible for carrying out society duties and maintaining institutional records. A faculty coordinator may advise the society and monitor compliance.

## 4. Elections
Elections shall be conducted according to the society's approved electoral procedure. Members eligible to vote should receive reasonable notice. Election records should be preserved for institutional review.

## 5. Meetings
The society should conduct meetings as required by its constitution. Minutes should identify the date, attendees, decisions, and responsible office bearers. Material decisions involving institutional resources should follow applicable approval procedures.

## 6. Events
Events using classrooms, auditoriums, equipment, or other institutional resources require the applicable booking and approval process. Organizers remain responsible for capacity, safety, and conduct requirements.

## 7. Finance
Society funds are institutional or member resources held for approved society purposes. Expenses should be supported by receipts and recorded. Personal expenditure by office bearers is not an approved use of society funds.

## 8. Reimbursements
Reimbursement requests should contain receipts and be submitted through the designated process. The approving authority may reject unsupported or unauthorized expenses.

## 9. Faculty Coordinator
The faculty coordinator may advise the society, review compliance, and refer serious concerns to the institution. The coordinator does not automatically possess authority to waive unrelated university academic requirements.

## 10. Conduct
Members must comply with institutional conduct requirements during society activities. Serious incidents may be referred to the appropriate disciplinary authority.

## 11. Amendments
The constitution may be amended through the process approved by the society and institution. Amendments should be recorded and communicated to members.

## 12. Dissolution
A society may be dissolved according to its approved constitution or by an institutional decision made under applicable rules. Remaining institutional funds and records must be handled according to the institution's directions.
