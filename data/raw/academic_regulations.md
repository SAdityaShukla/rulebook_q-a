# Academic Regulations and Student Policy Handbook

This institutional policy corpus is a synthetic university rulebook prepared for the Policy Proof evaluation. It contains academic, hostel, scholarship, finance, and student-organization rules. Section numbers are stable citation anchors.

### 1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
Every student enrolled in a theory course must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance during the semester to be eligible to sit the End Semester Examination. Attendance is calculated from the classes officially scheduled for the course. Students are responsible for checking attendance records regularly and reporting a suspected recording error to the course instructor within seven calendar days. Attendance below the minimum may result in the student being marked ineligible for the examination unless a specific exception is authorized elsewhere in these regulations.

### 1.2 Attendance Recording
Course instructors record attendance for each scheduled instructional meeting. A student is considered present only when the student attends the class for the required instructional period. Attendance cannot normally be transferred between courses. Students may request correction of an attendance entry by submitting supporting information to the instructor. The instructor should resolve a documented recording error before the examination eligibility list is finalized.

### 1.3 Short Attendance and Notice
When a student's attendance falls below the prescribed threshold, the department shall notify the student through the institution's normal academic communication channel where practicable. The notice should identify the course and the attendance percentage recorded at the time of review. A student may submit an explanation or supporting documents under the applicable leave, medical, or review procedure. Submission of an explanation does not by itself guarantee permission to sit the examination.

### 1.4 Laboratory Attendance
Students must maintain at least 80% attendance in laboratory or practical sessions to complete the laboratory requirement. The laboratory attendance requirement is mandatory and, under this clause, no exception or committee review is available for a student below 80%.

### 1.5 Medical Absence
A student who is absent from classes because of a documented medical condition may apply for medical consideration. The application should normally be submitted within five working days of returning to academic activity and should include documentation issued by a recognized medical practitioner or hospital. Approved medical consideration may permit the attendance shortfall to be reviewed by the designated academic authority. The authority may determine the extent to which approved medical absence is treated differently from ordinary absence. Medical consideration is not automatic merely because a student submits a certificate.

### 1.6 Examination Eligibility List
Before each End Semester Examination, the department prepares an eligibility list based on the latest verified attendance records. Students who are below a mandatory threshold and do not have an approved exception are listed as not eligible. A student who believes the list contains an error should contact the department before the published deadline. The examination cell may rely on the finalized list unless an authorized correction is communicated.

### 2.1 End Semester Examinations
The End Semester Examination is conducted at the end of the academic term according to the published examination schedule. Students must carry the required institutional identification and follow the examination cell's instructions. The examination schedule is published through official academic channels. A student is responsible for checking the date, time, and venue before the examination period.

### 2.2 Medical Examination Absence
If a student is unable to attend an examination because of a sudden medical emergency, the student should inform the department or examination cell as soon as reasonably possible and provide supporting medical documentation. Where the examination rules permit, the competent authority may consider a special examination, deferred assessment, or other remedy. A medical absence is assessed individually and is not equivalent to an ordinary decision to skip an examination.

### 2.3 Family and Personal Events
The academic regulations specifically describe medical absence procedures. They do not establish a general entitlement to a deferred or special examination for family weddings, vacations, social events, or other voluntary personal commitments. Students facing circumstances not expressly covered should consult the examination cell or academic authority, which may explain whether another applicable policy exists. Consultation should not be interpreted as a guarantee of an alternative examination.

### 2.4 Examination Conduct
Students must follow the examination cell's conduct requirements. Unauthorized materials, communication with another candidate, or use of prohibited electronic devices may be treated as an academic misconduct matter. The examination supervisor may report suspected violations for further review. Penalties, where applicable, are imposed under the institution's academic integrity or disciplinary procedures.

### 2.5 Revaluation
A student may request revaluation of an eligible written examination within the period announced by the examination cell. The request must identify the examination and course and must be accompanied by the prescribed fee where a fee applies. Revaluation does not guarantee an increase in marks. The revised result, if approved under the applicable process, is recorded through the official examination system.

### 2.6 Grade Improvement
Where an improvement examination is offered, eligibility and the maximum number of attempts are governed by the academic calendar and examination rules. Students should verify whether a particular course is included in the improvement scheme. An improvement attempt may replace or affect the earlier result only as specified by the published rules.

### 3.1 Academic Review Committee
The Academic Review Committee reviews petitions involving academic regulations where the committee has been granted authority under these rules. A committee decision must be recorded through the designated institutional process. The committee should consider the evidence supplied by the student and the relevant policy provisions. A committee cannot waive a requirement unless the regulations or another valid delegation expressly provide that authority.

### 3.2 Committee Waiver of Laboratory Attendance
The Academic Review Committee can waive the 80% laboratory attendance requirement in exceptional circumstances after reviewing documented evidence. A waiver should be recorded in writing and communicated to the relevant department and examination cell. This clause grants the committee authority to waive the laboratory threshold despite the general requirement in the laboratory attendance section.

### 3.3 Attendance Appeals
A student may appeal an attendance-related decision when the student believes the record was calculated incorrectly or an approved exception was not considered. The appeal should identify the course, the disputed attendance record, and the relevant supporting evidence. The appellate authority may uphold, correct, or remand the decision. A pending appeal does not automatically suspend examination eligibility unless the authority directs otherwise.

### 3.4 Departmental Authority
Departments administer attendance and academic records within the framework of university regulations. A department may publish procedural instructions that explain how students submit documents or check records, but such instructions cannot silently remove a substantive right or obligation established by a higher-level regulation.

### 4.1 Scholarships
Institutional scholarships may depend on academic performance, attendance, conduct, and financial criteria. Students should consult the current scholarship notice for deadlines and documentation requirements. A scholarship award is subject to verification of the information submitted by the student. Where a scholarship requires a minimum academic standing, the standing is assessed using the method specified in the scholarship rules.

### 4.2 Merit Scholarship
A merit scholarship may be awarded to students who meet the published academic performance criteria and any applicable attendance requirement. Selection is subject to verification and available scholarship capacity. Meeting the minimum criteria does not necessarily create an unconditional guarantee of an award if the scheme is competitive.

### 4.3 Scholarship Waivers
A student who receives an attendance waiver may remain eligible for scholarship consideration if all other scholarship conditions are satisfied. An attendance waiver should not automatically be treated as a disciplinary failure or academic disqualification. The scholarship committee may request documentation concerning the basis for the waiver.

### 4.4 Scholarship Review
Students may request review of a scholarship decision within the stated review period. The request should explain which criterion the student believes was applied incorrectly. The committee may ask the academic department for attendance or grade records. The committee's decision should be communicated through the official scholarship process.

### 5.1 Hostel Eligibility
Hostel accommodation is allocated subject to availability, institutional eligibility, and payment of the applicable hostel charges. Students must complete the prescribed allotment process and provide required information. Allocation may be withdrawn where a student loses eligibility or fails to comply with material hostel conditions.

### 5.2 Hostel Curfew
Residents must return to the hostel by the published curfew unless they have an approved late-entry arrangement. Repeated unauthorized late entry may result in disciplinary review. During examinations or special institutional events, the hostel administration may announce temporary changes to routine timings through official notices.

### 5.3 Hostel Leave
Students leaving the hostel overnight should complete the prescribed leave procedure. The leave request should state the dates and expected return. Emergency leave may be reported to the warden as soon as practicable. Students remain responsible for complying with any restrictions applicable to their residence category.

### 5.4 Hostel Guests
Visitors may enter designated hostel areas only according to the visitor procedure. A resident may be required to register a guest and comply with visiting hours. Guests are not permitted to stay overnight unless the hostel administration has specifically authorized the stay. Residents are responsible for their guests' compliance with hostel rules.

### 5.5 Hostel Rooms and Maintenance
Residents must keep rooms reasonably clean and report maintenance problems through the hostel maintenance process. Damage caused intentionally or through negligent misuse may be charged to the responsible resident after appropriate assessment. Residents should not make structural changes or install unauthorized fixtures in rooms.

### 5.6 Hostel Discipline
Smoking, prohibited substances, violence, harassment, and serious property damage are prohibited in hostel premises. Reported incidents may be referred to the appropriate disciplinary authority. The hostel administration may take immediate administrative steps needed to protect residents while a formal process is pending.

### 6.1 Fee Payment
Tuition and other institutional fees must be paid by the deadline published in the official fee schedule. Students are responsible for checking the applicable semester and fee component. Late payment may attract the late fee specified in the fee schedule. Failure to pay a required fee may restrict access to specified academic or administrative services.

### 6.2 Late Fee
Where the fee schedule specifies a weekly late charge, the charge begins according to the date and calculation method stated in that schedule. A student should retain the payment receipt. If the payment portal shows an amount inconsistent with the official schedule, the student should contact the accounts office before making a duplicate payment.

### 6.3 Fee Refund
Refunds are governed by the applicable withdrawal or cancellation procedure. A refund, when available, may depend on the date of withdrawal and the fee component involved. Certain administrative charges may be non-refundable. The accounts office processes approved refunds through the institution's designated payment mechanism.

### 6.4 Fee Extension
A student seeking an extension for payment should apply before the relevant deadline where practicable. An extension is discretionary unless another policy grants an entitlement. Approval should be documented. Students should not assume that submitting an extension request stops late-fee calculation unless the approval expressly says so.

### 7.1 Student Societies
A student society must operate according to its approved constitution and institutional procedures. The society should maintain a list of office bearers, submit required reports, and follow financial controls. Activities conducted in the institution's name may require prior approval depending on the type of event and use of institutional resources.

### 7.2 Society Elections
Society office bearers are elected according to the society constitution. The election process should provide members with the opportunity to participate under the rules applicable to that society. Election records should be retained for institutional review where required.

### 7.3 Society Funding
Society funds must be used for approved society activities and recorded in the society's financial records. Reimbursements require supporting receipts or other evidence accepted by the institution. Funds must not be used for personal expenses of office bearers.

### 7.4 Society Events
Society events requiring classrooms, auditoriums, equipment, or other institutional resources must follow the relevant booking and approval procedure. Organizers should submit event details within the lead time stated by the responsible office. Safety, capacity, and conduct requirements remain applicable to approved events.

### 7.5 Society Conduct
Society members must comply with institutional conduct rules while participating in society activities. The faculty coordinator may ask the society to address a conduct concern. Serious matters may be referred to the appropriate institutional disciplinary authority independently of the society's internal process.

### 8.1 Leave of Absence
Academic leave must be requested through the designated process. Approval depends on the category of leave and supporting evidence. A leave approval does not automatically modify every academic requirement; the student should check the specific effect of the approved leave on attendance, assessments, fees, and residence.

### 8.2 Internship Leave
Students undertaking an approved internship may receive academic attendance consideration when the internship has been authorized through the institution's internship process. The student must submit required internship documentation and comply with any assessment or reporting requirements. Internship consideration applies only to the period and courses covered by the approval.

### 8.3 Emergency Leave
Emergency circumstances should be reported to the department as soon as possible. The responsible authority may request supporting documentation and determine the appropriate academic procedure. Emergency leave does not automatically guarantee exemption from assessments or attendance requirements unless an applicable rule provides such relief.

### 9.1 Academic Integrity
Students are expected to submit original academic work and acknowledge sources appropriately. Plagiarism, fabrication, unauthorized collaboration, and other forms of academic misconduct may be investigated under the academic integrity procedure. Students should preserve drafts and supporting records where an assignment's authenticity could reasonably be questioned.

### 9.2 Disciplinary Process
Where a policy violation is formally investigated, the student should receive information about the allegation and the applicable opportunity to respond under institutional procedure. Interim administrative measures may be used where necessary, but a final disciplinary finding should follow the prescribed decision-making process.

### 10.1 Records and Corrections
Academic records should be maintained through official institutional systems. Students should use the designated process for corrections rather than relying on informal messages. When an authorized correction is made, the responsible office should update the official record and, where appropriate, notify affected offices.

### 10.2 Policy Interpretation
Where two provisions appear to conflict, the institution should interpret them in their applicable scope and context and, where necessary, refer the issue to the competent authority. A student-facing answer should identify the conflicting provisions rather than presenting an uncertain interpretation as a definitive rule.

### 10.3 No General Guarantee
A policy that is silent on a particular circumstance does not create an automatic entitlement. Students should not infer permission merely because a prohibition is not stated. Questions outside the scope of these documents should be directed to the responsible institutional office.

### 11.1 Communication
Official academic and administrative notices are communicated through designated institutional channels. Students should monitor those channels during the semester. A notice may establish a deadline or procedure only within the authority of the office issuing it and subject to higher-level regulations.

### 11.2 Exceptions
An exception to a rule must be supported by an express provision or valid authorization from an authority empowered to grant that exception. Informal assurances from individuals who do not possess delegated authority should not be treated as policy waivers.

### 12.1 Student Responsibilities
Students are responsible for reading applicable academic and administrative policies, meeting deadlines, retaining relevant records, and raising discrepancies promptly. Students are encouraged to seek clarification when a situation is not expressly addressed. Clarification is informational unless the competent authority issues a formal decision.

### 13.1 Attendance Audit Procedure
Departments conduct periodic attendance audits using the official attendance register. The audit compares scheduled instructional meetings with recorded presence and approved corrections. Students may inspect the records during the announced review period. A correction should identify the original entry, the reason for correction, and the authority approving the change.

### 13.2 Medical Documentation Review
Medical documents submitted for attendance consideration are reviewed for relevance to the period of absence. The reviewing authority may request clarification when dates or course impact are unclear. A document that merely establishes that a student visited a clinic does not necessarily establish inability to attend every missed class.

### 13.3 Examination Centre Rules
Students should report to the examination centre before the reporting time printed on the admit card. Late entry may be restricted according to examination instructions. Seating is assigned by the examination cell or supervisor. Students should follow all instructions concerning identification, permitted stationery, and submission of answer scripts.

### 13.4 Special Examination Requests
Requests for special examination arrangements should identify the examination concerned and the reason for the request. Medical requests should include appropriate documentation. The examination authority determines whether the circumstances fall within an approved category. The existence of a request does not itself create permission to miss the original examination.

### 13.5 Academic Progress
Students are expected to make satisfactory academic progress toward their programme requirements. Departments may review students with repeated failures, prolonged absence, or other academic difficulties. Academic support may include advising, remedial instruction, or a progress plan. Such support does not change published grading criteria unless an authorized decision says otherwise.

### 14.1 Scholarship Attendance Records
Where attendance is a scholarship criterion, the scholarship office should use verified academic records. If a documented waiver has been approved by a competent authority, the scholarship office should consider the waiver according to the applicable scholarship rule. Students should not submit conflicting attendance figures from informal sources.

### 14.2 Hostel Allocation Priority
Hostel allocation may consider programme requirements, year of study, distance from campus, and other criteria published for the relevant allocation cycle. Priority criteria can change through an official allocation notice. A previous year's priority order should not automatically be assumed to apply to a later year.

### 14.3 Hostel Safety
Residents must follow fire-safety instructions, keep emergency exits clear, and avoid unauthorized electrical modifications. Cooking or heating equipment may be restricted in rooms according to hostel safety notices. Residents should report hazards promptly to the warden or maintenance office.

### 14.4 Hostel Complaints
Hostel complaints should normally be submitted to the warden or designated grievance channel. The complaint should contain enough information for the administration to identify the issue. Retaliation against a student for making a good-faith complaint is not permitted under the general conduct framework.

### 14.5 Society Financial Reporting
Societies receiving institutional funds should maintain transaction records and submit financial statements at the end of the required reporting period. Missing receipts may delay reimbursement. The faculty coordinator may require clarification before approving an expenditure report.

### 14.6 Society Membership
Membership rules are governed by the approved constitution. A society should maintain transparent membership records and communicate material membership requirements to students. Membership disputes should first be handled through the society's constitutionally defined process, subject to institutional oversight.

### 15.1 Fee Calendar
The fee calendar published for a semester identifies the normal payment deadline and any applicable late-payment stages. Students should rely on the current official schedule rather than a previous semester's dates. Changes to deadlines should be communicated through an official notice.

### 15.2 Fee Disputes
A student who disputes a fee should preserve the invoice, payment receipt, and relevant notice. The accounts office may review the account and correct an administrative error. Filing a dispute does not automatically suspend a deadline unless the accounts office confirms an extension or hold.

### 15.3 Administrative Charges
Administrative charges may apply to services such as duplicate documents, late forms, or special processing when a current schedule authorizes the charge. A charge should be identifiable in the relevant notice or fee schedule. Students may request clarification when an amount is not identifiable.

### 15.4 Refund Timing
Approved refunds are processed after the responsible office verifies eligibility and account information. Processing time can vary by payment mechanism. Students should not treat the initiation of a refund request as confirmation that a refund has been approved.

### 16.1 Grievance Procedure
Students may raise academic or administrative grievances through the designated institutional grievance mechanism. A grievance should state the decision being challenged, the relevant dates, and the remedy requested. The reviewing body may seek records from the responsible department before deciding the matter.

### 16.2 Appeals and Escalation
An appeal should normally be filed within the period stated in the decision or governing policy. If the first reviewer lacks authority to resolve the issue, the matter may be escalated to the designated appellate authority. Escalation does not guarantee that the original decision will be changed.

### 16.3 Interpretation of Scope
A clause should be applied to the category of activity it expressly governs. A rule concerning laboratory attendance should not automatically be extended to theory attendance, and a hostel procedure should not automatically be treated as an academic examination rule. Where scope is unclear, the competent authority should clarify it.

### 16.4 Effective Dates
Policy amendments apply from the effective date stated in the amendment or official notice. A later amendment should not automatically be applied retroactively to a completed assessment unless the amendment expressly provides for such application and is legally or institutionally valid.

### 17.1 Fee Extension Authority
A fee extension may be approved by the Accounts Office when a student submits a request before the deadline and demonstrates a documented administrative difficulty. The extension, once approved, suspends late-fee calculation for the approved period.

### 17.2 Fee Extension Timing
Late fees continue to accrue throughout the extension period, including after an Accounts Office extension has been approved, until the end of the semester.

### 18.1 Scholarship Attendance Condition
For the Merit Scholarship, a student must have at least 75% attendance in every enrolled course at the time of scholarship selection. An attendance waiver does not alter this scholarship eligibility condition.

### 18.2 Scholarship Waiver Eligibility
A student with an approved attendance waiver may remain eligible for Merit Scholarship consideration provided the student meets the academic and conduct requirements. The waiver should be treated as satisfying the attendance condition for scholarship purposes.

### 19.1 Hostel Curfew
All hostel residents must return to their residence by 10:00 PM every day. A resident who returns after 10:00 PM without prior approval may receive a disciplinary warning.

### 19.2 Weekend Hostel Curfew
On Fridays and Saturdays, hostel residents may return by 11:30 PM without obtaining prior approval. The later weekend timing is a standing hostel rule and applies unless a temporary notice changes it.
